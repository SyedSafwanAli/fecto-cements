01 — The Company

Pakistan’s first anti-pollution cement manufacturing plant, and the first of its kind in South Asia.

Established at Sangjani, near Islamabad, the ISO 9001:2000 certified Fecto Cement Limited is one of the most integrated manufacturing units in the country, with a rated capacity to produce 780,000 tonnes of clinker per annum.

Fuller International Inc., USA erected the state-of-the-art technology plant at a total project cost of Rs. 2,104 million. Ever since the start of production, which commenced from January 01, 1990, Fecto Cement has an enviable track record of achieving maximum production with consistency.

05 — History

From Grant Road, Mumbai to Sangjani.

The roots of the company can be traced back to Coronation Footwear, a shoe store started by Ghulam Muhammad A. Fecto.

  1. Origins

    Coronation Footwear, Mumbai

    A shoe store started by Ghulam Muhammad A. Fecto in Mumbai on Grant Road.

  2. 1952

    Trading business established

    After partition he migrated from Mumbai to Dhaka and then in 1952 established a business involved in trading electrical goods, wires and home appliances.

  3. Japan

    First technical collaboration with Japan

    The company then made a pioneering effort as it entered into a joint venture with a Japanese brand for the manufacturing of Radio, which was the first-ever technical collaboration with Japan in Pakistan.

  4. 1975

    Diversification into industry

    The company began to diversify from trading to industrial activities. The group continued to move from strength to strength and over time manufactured Cement, Sugar, and Tractors.

  5. 1981

    Fecto Cement Limited established

    In 1981 Fecto Cement Limited was established.

  6. 1990

    Production begins

    Production commenced on January 1, 1990.

  7. Mid-90s

    Current management

    In the mid-nineties, the founder began to segment the Group with its companies being split amongst his children, of which he had 8, into wholly separate entities which no longer had any affiliation with each other outside of sharing a name.

    The responsibility of managing Fecto Cement Limited was entrusted to Mr. Mohammed Yasin Fecto and Mr. Asad Fecto who have continued to move from strength to strength with the company vision of building a better economic future for Pakistan.

06 — Environment & Safety

An anti-pollution plant, blessed with nature’s greenery.

At Fecto, environment protection and safety is taken as seriously as any other business objective. We understand that while economic growth is a must, our industrial processes should not affect the quality of life of mankind.

Simultaneously, we ensure the use of economically feasible energy for industrial production, which justifies our consideration for energy conservation. We also ensure technically safe management of hazardous waste in order to prevent environmental damage.
